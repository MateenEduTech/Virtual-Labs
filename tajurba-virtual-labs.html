About Us

Why a virtual lab?

Not every classroom has a telescope, a titration set, or a relief map — but every classroom with a screen can now have all three. TAJURBA was built to close that gap, one simulation at a time.

Learn anywhere

Every lab runs directly in a browser — on a school computer, a shared phone, or a home laptop — with nothing to install and nothing to buy.

No equipment needed

Glassware, globes, and government simulations — replaced with safe, repeatable, on-screen experiments that behave like the real thing.

Built for the syllabus

Each lab is mapped to a real subject taught in schools — Astronomy to Political Science — so classroom concepts get a hands-on counterpart.

The Developer

Dr. Mateen Yousuf

Teacher · School Education Department

Dr. Mateen Yousuf builds free digital learning tools for students who might otherwise never get to look through a telescope or run a chemistry experiment. TAJURBA is a one-person effort to bring nineteen hands-on laboratories to any classroom with a screen — designed, coded, and maintained independently as a contribution to public school education.
